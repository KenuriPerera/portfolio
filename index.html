02 — Reflective Journal

What I Learned in PPW

The Professional Pathways in Work module challenged me to examine my professional identity, communication style, and career direction with structured intentionality. Below are my key reflections across six core themes.

01

Discovering My Professional Identity

Self-Awareness

PPW introduced me to structured self-reflection for the first time. Through personality assessments and reflective exercises, I recognized that I naturally gravitate toward roles requiring both analytical thinking and interpersonal communication. I learned that my strength is not just in coding, but in understanding why a system needs to exist — the very foundation of Business Analysis.

02

The Power of Professional Communication

Communication

I had always considered myself a decent communicator, but PPW revealed how much nuance goes into professional communication — particularly in written form. Learning to structure emails, reports, and presentations with clarity and purpose has made me a more effective team member. I now think carefully about audience, tone, and intent before communicating in any professional context.

03

Understanding the Professional World

Workplace Readiness

One of the most valuable sessions focused on workplace expectations and culture. I realized that technical skills alone are insufficient — employers value adaptability, reliability, and the ability to work within team dynamics. Learning about professional ethics, workplace norms, and how to navigate organizational hierarchies gave me a realistic picture of what entering the workforce will look like.

04

From Vague Dreams to Structured Goals

Goal Setting

PPW taught me the SMART goal framework, which transformed how I think about my future. Instead of a vague ambition to "work in tech," I now have a structured career development plan with specific milestones, timelines, and action steps. This shift in thinking has already improved my academic planning and how I approach personal projects and internship preparation.

05

Crafting a Professional Narrative

CV & Personal Branding

Building my CV and LinkedIn profile during PPW was more challenging than I expected. I learned that a CV is not merely a list of facts — it is a carefully constructed narrative that positions you for specific opportunities. Understanding how to tailor content, highlight impact over activity, and present projects clearly was a practical skill I applied immediately in my internship applications.

06

Building a Lifelong Learning Mindset

Continuous Learning

The module consistently reinforced that the IT industry demands continuous upskilling. This motivated me to pursue the Postman API Fundamentals certification independently and take a more intentional approach to building skills beyond the formal curriculum. I now keep a personal learning log and set monthly micro-goals for professional development.
